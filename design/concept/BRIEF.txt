АНО «Центр социализации детей и молодежи "ДРУЖИМ"»

Малышева Ирина Анатольевна.

без барьеров и стереотипов.

Комплексная социализация и развитие навыков у детей и молодежи
для успешной интеграции в общество, укрепления семьи и создания

3. CENTRAL CREATIVE IDEA
────────────────────────────────────

The core visual metaphor is:

CONNECTION BETWEEN PEOPLE.

The name «ДРУЖИМ» should be visible in the visual language without
literally illustrating people holding hands.

Develop a recurring visual system based on:

- lines;
- paths;
- nodes;
- intersections;
- trajectories;
- separate paths gradually connecting;
- relationships between elements;
- subtle organic geometry.

The underlying idea:

a person does not exist alone;
family, specialists, peers and community create a network of support.

Do not explain this metaphor literally in copy.
Make it visible through layout, graphics and motion.

────────────────────────────────────
4. CHARACTER
────────────────────────────────────

The website should feel:

- contemporary;
- humane;
- calm;
- intelligent;
- visually distinctive;
- trustworthy;
- editorial;
- mature;
- emotionally strong without sentimentality.

It must NOT look like:

- a generic charity template;
- a generic NGO landing page;
- a SaaS startup;
- a kindergarten website;
- an AI-generated card grid.

Avoid:

- endless rounded cards;
- glassmorphism everywhere;
- generic gradient blobs;
- standard headline-left / image-right hero;
- random icon packs;
- excessive rounded rectangles;
- stereotypical charity blue;
- puzzle-piece symbolism;
- stock photos of unrealistically happy children;
- sad-child emotional manipulation;
- visual language based on pity;
- repetitive fade-up animation.

Use:

- strong typography;
- generous whitespace;
- asymmetric editorial composition;
- large-scale imagery where justified;
- restrained colors;
- deliberate visual rhythm;
- a recognizable line/path motif.

5. DESIGN EXPLORATION
────────────────────────────────────

Before choosing one design, explore THREE genuinely different creative
directions.

They must differ in more than colors.

For each direction establish:

- overall composition;
- typography;
- palette;
- line/node language;
- photography treatment;
- hero idea;
- section rhythm;
- motion character.

Create enough visual material to understand each direction.

Do not make three complete websites.
Create strong representative frames / boards.

Then compare the directions qualitatively against:

- suitability for «ДРУЖИМ»;
- trust;
- readability;
- distinctiveness;
- ability to support the connection metaphor;
- motion potential;
- mobile adaptability;
- risk of looking generic.

Do not use numeric scores.

Choose the direction with the strongest fit and explain why.
