# Content to verify

Do not invent values. Obtain and approve each item with the organization.

- Verified contact information.
- Social networks.
- Address and visiting details.
- Current groups and programs.
- Participant ages.
- Schedule.
- Participation conditions.
- Price or free-status information.
- Legal documents.
- Organization registration details.
- Real statistics and results.
- Approved photographs and usage rights.
- Privacy policy, consent, and form-processing requirements.
